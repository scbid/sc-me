Urgent Action Needed

Saddle Creek runs deep.
We will not pay for our own displacement.

A development proposal would demolish seven immigrant-owned Saddle Creek businesses for market-rate apartments. The final public hearing is June 2. We need emails to city council and the mayor before then.

Hearing Schedule

May 19, 2026 First public hearing, Omaha City Council, 2:00 PM
May 27, 2026 Second public hearing, Omaha City Council, 2:00 PM
June 2, 2026 Final hearing Final public hearing, Omaha City Council, 2:00 PM. Last chance for public testimony.

All hearings at Omaha City Council Chambers, Omaha-Douglas Civic Center, 1819 Farnam Street.

What to say

Copy and personalize this message. Adding your own words makes a real difference.

Sample email

Subject: Please oppose the demolition of Saddle Creek immigrant-owned businesses I am writing to urge you to oppose the development proposal that would demolish seven immigrant-owned businesses on the Saddle Creek corridor in favor of market-rate apartments. These are functioning, community-rooted businesses that were not meaningfully consulted and were not offered real alternatives. Demolishing them does not serve the neighborhood. It serves the interests of a developer at the expense of the people who have actually built this community. Saddle Creek does not need more market-rate apartments built on the graves of working businesses. It needs investment that supports what is already here. I am asking you to vote NO on this proposal and to require a genuine community benefits process before any similar development moves forward. Thank you. [Your name] [Your address or neighborhood]

Tip: The more specific and personal your email, the more weight it carries. If you know any of the businesses affected, mention them by name.
