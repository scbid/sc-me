Our Coalition

Two organizations. One community.

When merchants and residents stand together, we speak with one voice. This is not just a fight for working people. It is a fight for everyone who calls Saddle Creek home, everyone who shops here, eats here, and has built something here.

For Merchants

Saddle Creek Merchants United

We are the independent businesses of Saddle Creek, many family-owned for generations. We are required to fund the N. Saddle Creek BID through mandatory assessments, and we have seen nothing in return except disruption, closed streets, and zero transparency.

For Residents

Grammercy Park Collective

Founded in 2022 when residents found themselves shut out of existing processes. No formal hierarchy, no gatekeeping. Neighbors who come together around shared goals, working in solidarity with merchants and everyone affected by decisions made without community input.

The Situation

What is at stake in Saddle Creek

Our neighborhood is under pressure from multiple directions.

01

The N. Saddle Creek BID

Project 1. The N. Saddle Creek Business Improvement District is controlled by a developer who lives in Bennington and stands to profit. Local businesses pay mandatory assessments. There is no transparency, no community voice, and no accountability.

02

A New Tax District & Demolition

Project 2. A separate developer is seeking to create a new tax district at the south end of Saddle Creek. It would demolish seven longstanding immigrant-owned businesses to build market-rate apartments. Final hearing: June 2.

03

A Community Shut Out

Both projects were advanced without the voices of the people who live and work in Saddle Creek. Ordinances were pushed through while the mayor was out of town. We are deliberately excluded from decisions that affect our streets.
